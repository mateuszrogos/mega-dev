Ticket #4471 contains 412 unique reports, but the export includes 427 rows because 15 report IDs repeat in overlapping pages. All 412 unique reports were merged into #4471. The evidence separates them into two causes, not one.

Shared parser — 379 reports

The shared parser reads dates month-first. For tenants whose locale writes the day before the month, that reverses day and month. This group spans 11 tenants and app, email, and form reports.

Track the shared-parser follow-up in GitHub issue #1.

Outcome: Fixed in fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

Booking-v3 date hint — 33 reports

Booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY for day-first tenants, while the form interprets values using the tenant's locale. This group contains 33 form reports across Alder Physio (12), Copper Studio (11), and Pale Harbour (10). It is distinct from the shared-parser group.

Track the booking-v3 follow-up in GitHub issue #2.

Outcome: Not fixed; the booking-v3 form was retired.
